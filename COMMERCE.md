# Commerce and production

## Model
Shopify + Printify, print-on-demand, **no commercial inventory**. Production and shipping happen after the order. Purchase samples for approval; do not mistake samples for stocking inventory.

## Assortment rules
- **Core collection:** five artworks, all available as short-sleeve graphic tees.
- **Crewneck:** one or two **hero artworks** only; adapt artwork scale and placement for the heavier garment. Collection 001 candidates: Majorette and Swan Float.
- **Hoodie:** at most one design, potentially wordmark-only. Avoid automatically turning large art backs into streetwear merch.
- **Long sleeve:** skip at launch. Optional later wordmark-only with subtle sleeve symbol; not a mandatory extension.
- Do not expose every artwork × every garment combination merely because POD allows it.
- Each garment and colorway requires a distinct approved mockup, compatible variant, and margin check.

## Candidate blanks (verify before selling)
- Short-sleeve: **Comfort Colors 1717**, garment-dyed tee (not 717). Working anchor.
- Crewneck: **Comfort Colors 1566**, candidate; verify provider, price, colors and print areas.
- Hoodie: **Comfort Colors 1567**, candidate; verify provider, price, colors and print areas.
- Long-sleeve: **Comfort Colors 6014**, reference only; not launch assortment.
- Do not assume a color is shared across styles or providers. A color name alone does not prove an exact match.

## Custom neck branding: open operational issue
A branded neck is desirable. However, Printify inside-label eligibility is **product- and provider-specific**, often limited to compatible tear-away-label garments; the 1717 may have a sewn-in manufacturer's tag. Do not promise manufacturer-tag removal or an inside branded print until the chosen provider/variant confirms it. If unavailable, choose knowingly between keeping the manufacturer's tag, switching compatible blanks/providers, or postponing custom neck branding. Keep legal fiber/origin/care labeling compliant. Record any incremental label print charge **per garment**, not as a guessed universal number.

## Retail working hypotheses (not approved prices)
- Tee: **$45**
- Crewneck: test **$75+**, contingent on contribution margin
- Hoodie: test **$90+**, contingent on contribution margin
- Long sleeve: no launch price needed
- Test 2-/3-/5-tee bundles only after actual provider costs and combined shipping are verified. Discounts must preserve contribution. A collection may increase order size; adding designs alone does **not** lower POD unit costs.

## Unit economics
For each SKU record:
retail price
− base blank + fulfillment cost
− front print
− back print
− additional print areas
− verified custom label cost (or pending)
− merchant-absorbed shipping
− payment processing
− discounts
− estimated returns/reprints allowance
= **contribution before acquisition**;
then subtract attributable customer acquisition to estimate contribution after marketing.

Track per **order** as well as per **item**: multiple items may share acquisition and sometimes shipping, but shipping discounts depend on provider, product and destination. Model taxes separately as appropriate. Do not confuse gross margin with profit. Samples, Shopify/Printify subscriptions, photography and other fixed expenses sit below contribution.

## Printify verification protocol
Before artwork is finalized for a SKU, inspect the live Printify product and provider listing (or authorized API) and record: checked_at, source URL/product ID, provider, exact color name, variant/size availability, printing technique, print zones and max dimensions, base price, incremental print-area prices, shipping rates, neck-label eligibility and incremental price, fulfillment region and delivery estimates. Treat stale catalog values as unverified. For final mockups use the real garment reference and match the exact selected color. Do not invent color names or mark a stylized mockup as a product photograph.

## Production QA
Order samples of chosen garment/color/print combinations. Check silhouette, fabric, collar, wash behavior, color fidelity, print sharpness and feel, print placement, label, packaging, and delivery. Revisit margins after test orders. Accurate customer-facing product specifications are required.
