# PRIVATE PARADE
**Clothes worth looking at twice.**

Private Parade is an independent, collection-led art and apparel label. We make wearable artworks: beautiful at first glance, more interesting on a second look. Each release is a distinct visual world, not another variation of one fixed aesthetic.

## Brand constitution
1. **Art first, merchandise second.** Artwork should be desirable without the logo and strong enough to frame.
2. **Beauty before cleverness.** Unexpected, funny or strange details are welcome, never mandatory.
3. **Personality without gimmicks.** Confident, curious, sometimes irreverent; never generic California lifestyle, tourist souvenirs, or novelty merchandise.
4. **Collections, not templates.** Every release can change palette, typography, visual technique, subject matter and mood. Do not turn the 1970s orange-sunset look into a permanent house style.
5. **Taste over output.** One exceptional design is worth more than dozens of acceptable ones. Critique honestly and discard weak art.
6. **Wearability is creative direction.** Fit, color, collar, graphic scale, print quality and negative space matter as much as the isolated image.
7. **Collectibility without forced lore.** Five individually desirable pieces should form a coherent collection.
8. **Honest product language.** Accurately describe materials, origin, production and garment specifications.

## Identity
The PRIVATE PARADE name and distinctive brand sensibility are constants. The chunky upright serif PRIVATE PARADE / LOS ANGELES wordmark is the approved **Collection 001** reference; future collections may reinterpret typography, palette and decoration while maintaining a recognizable brand signature. No permanent palm-tree, LA imagery, vintage-print or surrealism mandate.

## Commercial approach
Zero commercial inventory: Shopify storefront and Printify print-on-demand. Working core tee price **$45**, subject to actual costs. Each collection comprises **five graphic tees**, with **one or two curated hero crewnecks**, **at most one hoodie** (possibly wordmark-only), and **no long sleeve at launch**. Release sporadically when the work merits it. Samples are necessary; commercial inventory is not.
