# Collection 001 — THE PARADE
**Status:** in creative development; only Majorette direction provisionally approved, not print/sample approved.
**Release:** unscheduled.

## Thesis
A public spectacle with nobody watching. Beautiful, cinematic, slightly lonely, quietly off-kilter: the ceremony continues even when the audience is gone. This is a **collection premise**, not a requirement to put literal parade imagery on every garment.

## Visual language — Collection 001 only
Warm, 1970s-inflected illustrated-poster sensibility; burnt orange sunset, butter yellow, cream, dark brown, faded teal, occasional deep navy. Confident hand-drawn linework and flat colors, rich visual personality with compositional restraint. Gently worn/softly broken artwork edges may integrate into the garment; avoid obvious sticker borders, splatter, artificial grunge and crowded filler. This palette and period treatment **do not define future collections**.

## Consumer-facing collection introduction — draft
A procession without an audience. The roads are empty, the lights are still on, and the show goes on anyway. Five illustrated pieces from a world that feels familiar until you look a little closer.

## 01 — Majorette (working title: The Wrong Shadow)
- **Visual:** warm orange-sunset boulevard with palms, one majorette walking away from viewer, baton raised, long evocative shadow. Strong cinematic mood and recognizable subject; avoid generic LA lifestyle treatment.
- **Front:** small left-chest upright chunky 1970s serif PRIVATE PARADE / LOS ANGELES, with a collection-appropriate decorative motif. Latest approved visual direction has a small palm accent; preserve recognizable wordmark structure.
- **Back:** orange-sunset version preferred over muted overhead street scene. **Medium-sized** graphic, approximately 25–35% smaller than the overly large earlier iteration, with generous fabric margin. Edges blend into ecru through deliberate illustrated shapes/soft broken ink, **not** a scalloped outline or hard rectangle.
- **Do not revert:** no huge full-back graphic, no busy map border, no monochrome aerial replacement, no stripping away the warm personality.
- **Status:** visual direction positively received; artwork still needs actual printable master, exact garment-color validation and sample review.
